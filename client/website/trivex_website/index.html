Who we are

About us

Trivex Industrial Solutions is a Sharjah-based industrial partner delivering the complete lifecycle — engineering, manufacturing, supply, installation and maintenance — for water and wastewater treatment, oil & gas, mining and heavy process industries.

From our SAIF Zone facility we fabricate, assemble and service a broad catalogue of process equipment, backed by control-panel manufacturing, valve supply and responsive on-site troubleshooting.
